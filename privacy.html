Privacy Policy

App: auto-post (operated by Veganism) · Last updated: 28 October 2026

auto-post is a private tool that the Veganism team uses to schedule and publish its own photo posts about veganism and animal welfare to its own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles when a TikTok account is connected to it.

1. Data we collect

When you connect a TikTok account through "Login with TikTok", we receive only what you approve on TikTok's permission screen:

  • Basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) — scope user.info.basic.
  • An access token and a refresh token that let the app upload and publish content to the connected account — scopes video.upload and video.publish.

We do not collect your password, messages, contacts, location, followers' data or any payment information.

2. How we use the data

  • To upload and publish photo posts (images and captions) that the account owner has prepared, to that same account.
  • To show which account is connected and whether a post was published successfully.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, as required to publish the posts you asked us to publish.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the server that runs the app, are never published, and are used only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to TikTok's official API.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while the account stays connected. You can disconnect at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → auto-post → Remove access. After disconnection, or on request by email, we delete the stored tokens and account information within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
